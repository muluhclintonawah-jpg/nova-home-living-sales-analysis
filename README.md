# Nova Home & Living — E-commerce Sales & Profitability Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how spreadsheet-based data analysis can help an e-commerce business understand its sales performance, product profitability and operational trends.

Using a fictional retail business, Nova Home & Living, I developed a sales analysis and reporting solution in Google Sheets, transforming raw transaction records into structured data, performance metrics and an executive dashboard.

The project demonstrates practical data analytics skills and the ability to turn business data into useful insights for decision-making.

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses need clear visibility into:

- Overall sales performance.
- Product-level revenue and profitability.
- Sales volume and customer purchasing activity.
- Profit margins and discount impact.
- Performance indicators that support business decisions.

The objective was to organise sales information and create a reporting solution that makes these areas easier to monitor.

## Tools Used

- Google Sheets
- Spreadsheet formulas
- Data cleaning and transformation
- KPI reporting
- Data visualisation
- Business analysis

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation and Cleaning

- Organised raw sales transaction records.
- Standardised inconsistent product names.
- Corrected inconsistent category entries.
- Structured the dataset for analysis.

### 2. Data Analysis

Calculated key business metrics, including:

- Sales Revenue
- Total Cost
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value

### 3. Dashboard Development

Created an executive dashboard featuring:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Best-Selling Product
- Reporting Period

### 4. Data Visualisation

Developed two charts:

- Revenue by Product
- Profit by Product

These visualisations help compare product performance.

## Key Business Insights

The analysis was structured to identify:

- Best-selling products.
- Products generating the highest revenue.
- Products contributing the most profit.
- Overall sales and profitability performance.

## Business Recommendations

The dashboard supports four practical recommendations:

1. **Prioritise profitable products:** Maintain adequate stock of products generating strong profit.
2. **Review discount strategy:** Evaluate whether discounts are reducing profit margins unnecessarily.
3. **Monitor product performance:** Track revenue, units sold and profit regularly.
4. **Improve inventory planning:** Use sales trends to guide future purchasing decisions.

## Project Deliverables

- Raw Sales Data
- Cleaned Data
- Executive Dashboard
- Product Performance Summary
- Business Insights and Recommendations

## Project Limitations

This is a portfolio demonstration project built using fictional sales data. It does not represent a real client engagement or actual business performance.

The results are intended to demonstrate analytical methods and reporting capabilities.
