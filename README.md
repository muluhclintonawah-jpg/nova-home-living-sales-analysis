# Nova Home & Living — E-commerce Sales & Profitability Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how spreadsheet-based data analysis can help an e-commerce business understand sales performance, product profitability and business trends.

Using fictional sales data for Nova Home & Living, I developed a sales analysis and reporting solution in Google Sheets. The project transforms raw transaction records into structured data, calculates key performance indicators and presents the results through an executive dashboard.

The objective is to demonstrate practical data analytics skills and the ability to turn business data into useful information for decision-making.

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses need clear visibility into:

- Overall sales performance
- Product-level revenue and profitability
- Sales volume
- Profit margins and discount impact
- Key performance indicators that support business decisions

This project demonstrates how organised sales data and clear reporting can help address these needs.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation and Cleaning

- Organised raw sales transaction records.
- Standardised inconsistent product names.
- Corrected inconsistent category entries.
- Structured the dataset for analysis.

### 2. Data Analysis

Calculated key business metrics, including:

- Sales Revenue
- Total Cost
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Average Order Value

### 3. Executive Dashboard Development

Created an executive dashboard presenting:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Best-Selling Product
- Reporting Period

### 4. Data Visualisation

Developed charts to communicate:

- Revenue by Product
- Profit by Product

## Business Insights

The dashboard is designed to help decision-makers identify:

- Best-selling products
- Highest-revenue products
- Most profitable products
- Overall sales and profitability performance

## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritise profitable products:** Maintain adequate stock of products generating strong profit.
2. **Review discount strategy:** Evaluate whether discounts are reducing profit margins unnecessarily.
3. **Monitor product performance:** Regularly track revenue, units sold and profit.
4. **Improve inventory planning:** Use sales trends to guide future purchasing decisions.
